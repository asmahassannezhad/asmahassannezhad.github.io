# Content migration record

Source website: [asmahassannezhad.wordpress.com](https://asmahassannezhad.wordpress.com/). Retrieved directly on 9 September 2026. Search-engine copies were older than the live research page; the migration uses the live page.

## Included

- Three core pages: About, Research and Teaching.
- One preprint, including *Lower bounds for the low Steklov eigenvalues* (May 2026); 18 peer-reviewed or accepted papers; two other publications.
- Coauthor credits in the publication citations. The separate collaborators directory has been removed at the owner’s request.
- All five dated Bristol teaching entries and all four invited mini-courses.
- Both original lecture-note PDFs, stored locally in the package.
- The five original photographs at their legacy WordPress paths for incoming-link compatibility. Photographs are not displayed in the new design.
- Public contact information and links to arXiv, Google Scholar, ORCID and the Bristol profile.

The files under `wp-content/uploads/2021/09/` duplicate the original image and PDF paths so those paths remain valid on the new site. Do not remove them if you rely on incoming links or later redirect WordPress traffic.

## Editorial decisions

- The title is updated to **Associate Professor in Mathematics**, as listed by the [University of Bristol](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/persons/asma-hassannezhad/).
- Teaching entries with dates through 2024 are presented as **past teaching**. A 2026–27 teaching assignment has not been inferred.
- The Robin nodal-count paper retains the live site's “to appear” status. The 2025 hyperbolic-surfaces and tubes papers use publication information from the live site and Bristol's profile.
- The volume for *Eigenvalues of the Laplacian and extrinsic geometry* is corrected from the original site's “4” to **44**, confirmed by the [publisher](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10455-013-9379-8).
- Journal names and punctuation are made consistent. Public arXiv and publisher links are retained. Institutional-proxy links decorating the Escobar journal citation are removed; the citation itself is retained.
- Historical event links are preserved from the original site. Their current availability has not been exhaustively checked.
- No new awards, biography claims, open positions, student testimonials or CV have been invented.
- WordPress comments, account widgets, subscriptions, advertising and autogenerated attachment pages are outside the three-page content migration.
